Who we are

People-centered development that starts where need is greatest.

Shamaj Unnayan Foundation brings together volunteers, experts, and local leaders to reduce poverty, strengthen rural communities, and create sustainable pathways for long-term social change.

From youth training to healthcare access and environmental action, we design programs that solve real challenges with a local lens and measurable outcomes.
